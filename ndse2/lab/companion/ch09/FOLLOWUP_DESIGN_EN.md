Use independent task authors who did not create the memory card or scoring rules. Fix the repository snapshot, retrieval budget and access rules. Compare raw-repository and memory-supported conditions on new evidence-location and explanation tasks.

Counterbalance task/condition order for a human within-person design only where carryover can be controlled; otherwise randomize participants. Use different tasks for practice and assessment, blinded scoring with prespecified anchors, source and version checks, and participant-level uncertainty.

Keep any LLM-agent cohort separate, pin its model/tool versions, and do not count repeated model runs as human participants. Record missing answers and unauthorized assistance. This is a proposed protocol, not an executed human or agent-reasoning evaluation.
