# Proposed mentoring scene — no participants observed
One learner, one senior and an assistant; no assumption that role count causes learning.
1. Learner predicts historical replay and cites authority before viewing a generated answer.
2. Assistant presents the supplied replay mutant (or a mentor does so in the offline exercise).
3. Senior asks for the contradictory step and an independently grounded counterexample.
4. Learner repairs and tests, then explains why the current policy must not rewrite history.
5. Senior records reasoning feedback and unresolved assumptions, not only diff approval.
Record prompts, learner explanation, code/test result and later assessment separately.
This scene is a design option for the existing assisted-work protocol, not a third study arm.
