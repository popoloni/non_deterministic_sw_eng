# Proposed real pilot (NOT YET CONDUCTED)

Population: bounded, comparable maintenance tasks in one repository family; exclude production secrets and high-risk live transactions. Randomize eligible tasks within risk/task-family strata to human-led, pair, bounded-agent and agent-plus-independent-checker modes. Avoid model contamination of independent acceptance criteria.

Primary endpoint: total observed engineering cost per independently accepted change over a common 30-day follow-up. Secondary endpoints: released changes, developer and reviewer minutes, rejection, rework, rollback, incidents, time to release and policy violations. Track every assigned task, including failures and abandonment.

Design safeguards: pre-register exclusions and hard safety gates, independently score acceptance, record task-level assignment, and cluster uncertainty by developer/team where needed. Do not set a sample size without a variance estimate or meaningful effect threshold. Use a pilot for variance estimation first.

Operational prerequisites: an actual engineering team, consent/organizational authorization, a task backlog, model/tool access, telemetry, reviewer assignment, and a release/incident observation window. None are available in this chat.
